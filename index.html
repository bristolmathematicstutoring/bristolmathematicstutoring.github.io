Our approach

We can go through problem-solving, the school curriculum (GCSE, A-LEVEL), university entrance exams, preparation for university level mathematics, help with current undergraduate courses or mathematics beyond for professional work. We can facilitate extracurricular projects for interested students. Tutoring will be tailored to your interests and goals. Our approach in teaching is student lead, offering the freedom to learn at your own pace. Sessions will be relaxed and non-judgemental and will focus on engaging with the necessary mathematical content to help you progress. We'll engage with concepts, particular examples and problem solve. Both of us are looking forward to starting this journey with you!

About Us

Elliot

For the past 5 years I have led analysis tutorials and support sessions in first order logic and model theory at the University of Bristol. I specialise in analysis and geometry, with a background in measure theory, differential geometry and mathematical logic. In my teaching I emphasise the fundamentals: understanding how mathematical proofs are constructed and developing intuition for core definitions and theorems through motivating examples.
